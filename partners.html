Partners

The fine-scale study of coastal risks relies on synergies between fundamental research and field stakeholders. Senti-Risk collaborates closely with key academic and non-profit partners who actively contribute to acquiring and sharing biological tracking data.

Biosphère Environnement

A non-profit research institute founded by ecologists and biologists on the Gironde estuary, BioSphère Environnement specialises in conservation biology and coastal wetland studies. The organisation is a key partner of Senti-Risk for the biotelemetry tracking of Atlantic avifauna, as well as for delivering scientific outreach and educational programmes in schools.

Bretagne Vivante

Nature conservation charity and manager of protected areas in Brittany. Through its Larus research programme, focused on the demographic monitoring and biotelemetry of large gulls, the organisation collaborates with Senti-Risk by sharing GPS tracking data to analyse fisheries interactions and, more broadly, bird exposure to cumulative coastal risks.

GISOM (Scientific Interest Group for Marine Birds)

National research network dedicated to knowledge and conservation of seabirds across mainland France and overseas territories. By pooling scientific expertise and long-term monitoring from its members, GISOM collaborates with Senti-Risk to provide an integrated overview of population status facing anthropogenic pressures and environmental changes along the coast.

LIENSs (Littoral, Environment and Societies)

Joint research unit (CNRS / La Rochelle University) dedicated to interdisciplinary coastal studies. Through its BIOFEEL team (Biodiversity and Functioning of Coastal Ecosystems), the laboratory collaborates with Senti-Risk on avian community dynamics, trophic ecology, and bird responses to coastal pressures and risks.

LPO (League for the Protection of Birds)

Leading national conservation charity dedicated to biodiversity protection (BirdLife partner in France). Drawing on its local teams in Charente-Maritime and its network of coastal nature reserves (including the Lilleau des Niges Nature Reserve), the LPO is a key partner of Senti-Risk for avian telemetry tracking and land-sea interaction modelling.

Migratlane

Major national scientific project aimed at characterizing migratory pathways and trajectories of seabirds and coastal birds across the English Channel and Atlantic seaboard. As a partner of Senti-Risk, the consortium integrates its biotelemetry data to assess spatial connectivity and interaction risks with human activities both offshore and onshore.

Tour du Valat

Research foundation dedicated to the conservation and sustainable management of wetlands. As a partner of Senti-Risk, the institute contributes biotelemetry data on Mediterranean gull populations, essential for characterizing bird movements and land-sea interactions at an interregional scale.
